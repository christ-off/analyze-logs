# Googlebot vs 410 (Gone) pages

Question: does Googlebot stop requesting URLs that return 410, or keep retrying?

## Replay

```bash
sqlite3 -header logs.db "
-- 410 hits per day
select substr(timestamp,1,10) day, count(*) hits, count(distinct uri_stem) urls
from cloudfront_logs where user_agent like '%Googlebot%' and status=410
group by 1 order by 1;

-- hits per URL
select uri_stem, count(*) n, min(timestamp), max(timestamp)
from cloudfront_logs where user_agent like '%Googlebot%' and status=410
group by uri_stem order by n desc;

-- Googlebot status mix per day
select substr(timestamp,1,10) day, status, count(*)
from cloudfront_logs where user_agent like '%Googlebot%'
group by 1,2 order by 1,2;"
```

Note: `LIKE '%Googlebot%'` includes spoofed UAs (IPs not verified).

## Result (2026-09-29, data from 2026-06-29)

| Day | 410 hits | URLs |
|---|---|---|
| 09-25 | 2 | 2 |
| 09-26 | 8 | 8 |
| 09-27 | 12 | 12 |
| 09-28 | 11 | 11 |
| 09-29 (partial) | 10 | 10 |

- 17 URLs returned 410 to Googlebot: 12 hit 3x, 2 hit 2x, 3 hit 1x (2 were probes: `/google-service-account.json`, `/debug/pprof`).
- Max one hit per URL per day, no hammering.
- 410 started ~09-25; Googlebot 404s dropped 20 -> 1 per day over the same period.
- Book URLs are double-encoded (`%25C3%25A8`), which may be why they are 410.

## Conclusion

Too early to say 410 is useless: Googlebot rechecks a few times by design and the window is 4 days.
Replay in 2-3 weeks: if daily 410 hits are still ~10 on the same URLs, 410 isn't reducing crawls.
Also check Search Console > Pages ("Not found (404)" / "Gone") for index removal.
